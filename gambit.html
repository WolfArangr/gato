GAMBIT
Chess Tower Defense

The black army marches by the rules of chess — and it captures. Place white pieces that strike exactly as they would on a real board. Hold for twenty waves.

How to play

The game
1
Black pieces enter on the top rank and move exactly like chess pieces toward your King. Any black piece that attacks him — or stands next to him — costs crowns. Lose them all and it's checkmate.
2
Tap a piece in the tray, tap a square to preview it, tap again to place. Or drag it straight onto the board. Red crosses show squares black is attacking.
3
Both armies fight. Black attacks your pieces and captures them by moving onto their square. Pieces left alone for a few seconds slowly heal, and all heal fully when a wave is cleared.
4
Black pawns only step straight down and capture diagonally — block one and it's stuck. A pawn that reaches your back rank promotes to a Queen.
5
Your pawns earn a promotion after 5 captures. The hourglass, Stalemate, freezes the whole black army for a few seconds.
