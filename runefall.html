How to play

Point with the mouse, or drag a finger anywhere across the board to swing the engine round. Lift your finger — or tap once — to loose the orb.

Line up three or more orbs of one colour to shatter them. Gaps close on their own — when they close on a matching colour, the chain keeps breaking and your score multiplies.

Tap the war engine itself to trade the loaded orb for the next one — or use the swap rune, the space bar, or right-click.

Clear every orb before the line reaches the abyss.

Large breaks drop a relic onto the floor. Shoot it to claim it: frost halts the march, recoil drives the line back, blast shatters everything nearby, and purge banishes every orb sharing your loaded colour.

Clear without missing and your multiplier climbs. Threading a shot through a gap in the line pays a bounty.
