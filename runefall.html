How to play

Aim with the pointer or your finger. Tap anywhere to loose an orb from the ballista.

Line up three or more orbs of one colour to shatter them. Gaps close on their own — when they close on a matching colour, the chain keeps breaking and your score multiplies.

Press the swap rune (or the space bar, or right-click) to exchange the loaded orb for the next one.

Clear every orb before the line reaches the abyss.
